Privacy Policy

Application: UgO NITK (in.ugo.nitk.mobile) Developer: UgO Cycle Sharing Team Effective Date: October 10, 2026

1. Introduction & Overview

Welcome to UgO NITK, a community-driven peer-to-peer bicycle sharing platform developed by the UgO Cycle Sharing Team exclusively for students, staff, and faculty of the National Institute of Technology Karnataka (NITK), Surathkal.

Your privacy is paramount to us. This Privacy Policy discloses our practices concerning the collection, storage, processing, and transfer of personal and device information when you use our mobile application and related services. By downloading, accessing, or using the UgO NITK mobile application, you consent to the data collection and usage practices described herein.

Core Promise: We collect only the minimum data required to facilitate safe cycle rentals, authenticate campus identities, process transactions, and coordinate meetups within the NITK Surathkal campus. We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal data to any third-party advertisers or data brokers.

2. Information We Collect

To deliver a secure and verified campus cycle-sharing service, we collect the following categories of information:

A. Information You Provide Directly

  • Identity & Account Details: Full Name, institutional email address (e.g., @nitk.edu.in), contact phone number, student roll number/department, and campus hostel/residence address.
  • Cycle Listings & Photos: Brand, model, cycle type, photos of the cycle, lock combination notes, and hourly/daily rental pricing specified by cycle owners.
  • Identity & Verification Proof: Cycle condition photos captured upon ride pickup and upon ride return to substantiate cycle integrity and prevent disputes.
  • Ratings & Reviews: Qualitative reviews and star ratings submitted post-ride for cycles and peer renters/owners.

B. Automatically Collected Information

  • Device & Network Data: Android OS version, device model, hardware identifiers, network carrier, application version, and diagnostic crash logs.
  • Push Notification Tokens: Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM) registration tokens used to deliver operational rental updates, OTP codes, and incoming audio call notifications.

5. Data Security & Integrity

We implement comprehensive technical, physical, and administrative safeguards to protect your personal information:

  • Encryption in Transit: All communications between the UgO mobile app and backend servers utilize industry-standard Transport Layer Security (TLS 1.2/1.3) with HTTPS and secure WebSockets (WSS).
  • Tokenized Authentication: Passwords are cryptographically hashed using salted bcrypt routines. API requests are authenticated using short-lived JSON Web Tokens (JWT).
  • Restricted Access: Access to user logs and administrative portals is strictly limited to authorized UgO NITK administrators under multi-factor authentication.

6. Data Retention & Account Deletion Policy

In compliance with Google Play's Account Deletion Requirement, UgO provides clear, accessible methods for all users to request permanent deletion of their account and associated data.

Data Retention Schedule

  • Active Profiles: Retained as long as your UgO account remains active and in good standing.
  • Location & Telemetry: Ephemeral ride telemetry is purged or anonymized within 30 days following rental completion.
  • Financial & Transactional Records: Transaction IDs, payment references, and rental receipts are retained for 7 years in accordance with Indian tax laws and the Prevention of Money Laundering Act (PMLA).

How to Delete Your Account & Associated Data

You may initiate an account deletion request through either of the following methods:

  1. In-App Deletion: Navigate to Profile → Account Settings → Request Account Deletion in the mobile app.
  2. Web-Based Deletion Request: Visit our dedicated Account Deletion Request Portal or email us directly at ugocyclesharing@gmail.com with your registered NITK email and Roll Number.

Timeline: Account deletion requests are verified and processed within 7 business days. Upon deletion, your profile details, contact number, uploaded cycle photos, and verification documents are permanently purged from active databases.
