Account & Data Deletion Request

Users of the UgO NITK mobile app have the right to request the permanent deletion of their account and all associated personal records at any time.

Data Deletion & Retention Overview

In strict compliance with the Google Play Developer Account Deletion Policy, this page details the steps to request account deletion, what personal data will be purged, and what minimal records must be retained under applicable statutory laws.

✕ Data Permanently Deleted

  • Full Name, NITK Email Address, and Phone Number
  • Campus Hostel, Room, and Roll Number Details
  • Authentication credentials and password hashes
  • Uploaded cycle images and listing descriptions
  • Cycle verification pickup/return photographs
  • Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM) push tokens
  • Live telemetry, location traces, and ride routes

⚠ Data Retained (Statutory Requirements)

  • Razorpay Payment Records & Receipts: Transaction references, rental fees, and security deposit settlements are retained for 7 years strictly in accordance with Indian tax statutes and financial regulations.
  • Anonymized Aggregate Stats: Non-identifiable usage metrics (e.g., total campus rides completed) retained for academic research and carbon-reduction reporting.

Processing Timeline: Once submitted and verified against your registered email, all deletion requests are permanently executed within 7 business days.

How to Request Account Deletion

Method 1: In-App Request (Recommended)

If you have the UgO NITK app installed on your Android device:

  1. Open the UgO NITK application.
  2. Tap on the Profile tab in the bottom navigation.
  3. Scroll down and tap Account Settings → Request Account Deletion.
  4. Confirm your request. Your account will immediately enter a scheduled purge queue.
